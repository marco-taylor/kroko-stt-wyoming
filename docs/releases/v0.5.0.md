# v0.5.0 — German Community-128 blank penalty

German Community-128 now uses modified beam search with 4 active paths and
`blank_penalty=0.75`. Classic and Community-64 retain greedy search. Other
languages and custom models retain their existing defaults.

True online streaming, audio processing, endpointing and model weights are
unchanged. Existing Community-128 models are reused without downloading new
weights.

On 862 distinct FLEURS-DE recordings, the previously released Beam-4 decoder
improved WER from **8.102% to 7.188%**, an **11.28% relative improvement**.
The additional blank penalty was selected using a separate development set and
evaluated on its confirmation split. Penalty 0.75 reached **7.1139% WER** and
**4.2495% CER** there, compared with 7.2115% WER and 4.3386% CER for penalty
0.50. The incremental advantage over 0.50 is small and not clearly statistically
established. The confirmation results are not a direct comparison with the
862-recording aggregate. These benchmark results do not guarantee gains on every
recording. No language model, hotwords or experimental bindings are included.
